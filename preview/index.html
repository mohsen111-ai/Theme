Nyx

A night-sky theme for the Infinix GT 30 Pro: hand-inked wallpapers, sketched app icons on a home screen of its own, and a harpy who watches over all of it. This page shows the drawing style before the full set of 300 gets made.

The harpy

Nyx's mascot. Fierce, winged, taloned. Here she is perched, gliding and mid-stroke. She turns up on rooftops, cliffs and across the moon in the wallpapers.

App icons

Twelve of the sketched icons. The finished pack covers the apps most phones have and falls back to a hand-drawn tile for anything else.

Wallpapers

Tap one to see it full size. Live and Long scenes are moving right now. Long scenes run a 45 second cycle, so wait a moment to see the sky change.

The full set

Every scene is drawn by the same engine, so the full set is mostly new compositions of the same parts: moon, houses, trees, water, weather, creatures.
